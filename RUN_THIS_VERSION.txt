GOOGLE COST RULE:
    Google Routes is used ONLY for the selected week.
    Future weeks can influence clustering and drawing priority but do not trigger
    Google route calculations.

Availability also scales the detailed route shortlist:
    5 days ≈ 40 candidates
    3 days ≈ 24 candidates
    2 days ≈ 16 candidates
    1 day  ≈ 8 candidates
when the default five-day cap is 40.
