VERSION 9 — AI WEEKLY PLANNER

Run:
    pip install -r requirements.txt
    streamlit run app.py

You should see:
    Version 9 — AI weekly planner with future-cluster reasoning

Add these to Streamlit Secrets:
    GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY
    OPENAI_API_KEY
    OPENAI_MODEL
    TFL_API_KEY
    MET_OFFICE_API_KEY
    MET_OFFICE_GLOBAL_SPOT_URL

The AI ranks current-week sites and can lower the priority of a site when its
postcode district forms a materially stronger cluster in a later week.
Google Routes and the deterministic scheduler still enforce the actual travel
and 07:50–16:00 working-day constraints.
